BENZER — local image folder
============================

Drop these files here (exact, lowercase names). Paths are configured in
src/data/content.js. Missing files show the branded fallback panel.

  hero.jpg             Landscape, >= 2400px wide. Subject in upper-centre;
                       text sits bottom-left, so keep that area calm/dark.
  brand-story.jpg      Portrait 4:5, >= 1400px tall.
  formal-edit.jpg      Portrait 3:4, >= 1600px tall (tall feature tile).
  everyday-luxury.jpg  3:4 or 4:5, >= 1200px. Cropped portrait on mobile,
                       landscape on tablet - keep the subject centred.
  occasion-wear.jpg    3:4 or 4:5, >= 1200px. Also used (darkened, greyscale)
                       behind the brand statement section.
  product-1.jpg        Blazer. Portrait 3:4, >= 1200px tall. Also used as the
                       small detail inset in the brand story section.
  product-2.jpg        Oxford shirt. Portrait 3:4.
  product-3.jpg        Tailored trousers. Portrait 3:4.
  product-4.jpg        Eveningwear. Portrait 3:4.

Tip: export as JPG at ~80% quality; aim for < 500 KB each (hero < 900 KB).
To fine-tune a crop, edit that image's `position` value in content.js
(e.g. 'center 20%' moves the focus upward).
